# Escalation Rules

## When to escalate
Escalate a claim to human review when any of the following is true:

- A **fraud indicator** is present on the claim.
- The claim **amount exceeds EUR 5,000**.
- The computed **risk level is HIGH**.
- **Required documents are missing**.
- The **policy is not active**.

## Fraud
- Any claim with a fraud indicator must be escalated and must never be
  auto-processed.
- Suspicious claim history increases the risk score and can raise the risk level.
