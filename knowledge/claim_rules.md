# Claim Processing Rules

## Eligibility for automatic processing
A claim may be processed automatically (straight-through processing) only if ALL
of the following hold:

- The associated policy is **ACTIVE**.
- All **required documents** for the coverage are present.
- No **fraud indicator** is set on the claim.
- The claim **amount is at or below the auto-processing limit** of EUR 5,000.
- The computed **risk level is LOW**.

## Amount thresholds
- Claims **<= EUR 5,000** are eligible for automatic processing.
- Claims **> EUR 5,000** require human approval.

## Coverage
- Accidental Medical Expense claims are payable up to the policy limit, after the
  policy deductible has been applied.
